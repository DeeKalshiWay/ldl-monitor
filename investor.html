Track record

Oil Service Fuels, Inc. has manufactured Longer Diesel Life® continuously since 1981 — 45 years across multiple diesel-fuel regulatory regimes (low sulfur → ULSD → biodiesel blends) without a formula recall, EPA enforcement action, or class-action liability event.

LDL is sold direct from the manufacturer through founder-led commercial relationships. The company holds an active EPA fuel-additive registration under 40 CFR Part 79 and has historically targeted bulk-fuel buyers across autos, pickups, heavy construction, mining, agriculture, generators, locomotives, and marine.

Risk factors

Revenue concentration

LDL sales are concentrated across a relatively small commercial customer base. Loss of a major account would materially affect distributions.

Regulatory / EPA

Changes to EPA fuel-additive registration requirements, ULSD spec, or biofuel-blending mandates could affect formula compatibility.

Competitive displacement

Roll-up activity in the additive space (Aurora Capital / Lubrication Engineers consolidation) may compress fleet-channel pricing through 2026-2028.

OEM endorsement gap

LDL does not currently hold Cummins / CAT / Detroit Diesel formal endorsements. Procurement-driven fleets may require these. Active mitigation: pursuing independent L-10 / N-14 / BOCLE testing.

Founder concentration

Operations are tightly coupled to a single principal (Alan Epler, President). Succession and key-person risk should be documented in offering disclosures.

Token liquidity

Secondary-market trading depends on a regulated ATS. Liquidity is expected to be substantially below public equities. 12-month lockup applies.

Audit cadence

Revenue distributions depend on accurate, timely accounting. Annual audit is planned; investors should understand the reporting cadence before subscribing.

Tokenization regulatory evolution

SEC enforcement and Treasury guidance on RWA tokenization continue to evolve. Future changes may affect transferability or accredited-investor scope.
